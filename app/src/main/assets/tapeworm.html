The microphone continuously grows new segments. Segment length is also the repeat delay: a 200 ms segment advances to its next loop every 200 ms. Each segment fades proportionally across its selected number of loops, then drops off the end of the worm.

CAPTURE → LOOP → FADE → SHED.

Every segment is immutable PCM after capture. Its duration is also its playback delay. If PITCH makes a segment finish early, Tapeworm tiles that same pass until the next delay boundary; if it runs long, the plays overlap naturally. LOOPS defines the falloff: 4 loops play at 100%, 75%, 50%, 25%, then shed.
