The microphone continuously grows immutable segments. SEGMENT controls how much live audio is captured per piece. PITCH controls how fast that piece itself plays. LOOP DELAY controls when the next repeat begins relative to the piece's full playback time at the current pitch.

CAPTURE → LOOP → FADE → SHED.

At LOOP DELAY 1.00×, every repeat begins exactly when one full playback of that segment would finish at the current PITCH. Below 1.00× repeats overlap; above 1.00× leaves space between repeats. LOOPS defines proportional falloff all the way to zero, up to 256 passes.
