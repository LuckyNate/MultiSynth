The worm is one continuous four-second PCM loop. SEGMENT only divides that loop into teeth; changing it never rebuilds or clears the recorded audio.

4 SECOND WORM // VARIABLE TEETH.

Segment count is derived from loop length ÷ segment length. 50 ms gives 80 teeth; 80 ms gives 50; 100 ms gives 40; 125 ms gives 32. Recording is always real-time 1×. Playback is relative to that recording, and 0.4× stretches the four-second loop to the ten-second maximum.
