The microphone continuously grows immutable segments. SEGMENT sets how much live audio is captured per piece, from 50 to 500 ms. PITCH changes how fast each piece plays. LOOP LENGTH is the absolute time from one repeat start to the next, from 500 ms to 20 seconds.

CAPTURE → LOOP → FADE → SHED.

The ranges meet at 500 ms: no captured segment can ever be longer than the shortest loop. LOOP LENGTH remains absolute; LOOPS defines proportional falloff to zero over as many as 64 passes.
