PLAY 1.000×80 ms // 4 LOOPS

The microphone continuously grows new segments. Each finished segment loops a fixed number of times, fades proportionally with each pass, then drops off the end of the worm. Playback is never recorded back into the microphone path.

CAPTURE → LOOP → FADE → SHED.

Every segment is immutable PCM after capture. LOOPS defines its entire life and its falloff: 4 loops play at 100%, 75%, 50%, 25%, then the segment is discarded. New segments keep arriving continuously behind it.
